Why this isn't prompting advice

"Write better prompts" doesn't scale.

This is an operating-model problem: who designs the loop, who governs it once it's reused, who's accountable for what it costs when it runs a thousand times instead of once.

Demo 2 — Govern

Pinned, and gated by policy.

  1. The reusable loop from Demo 1 ships as a versioned skill — plugin.json pins it, not a promise to remember.
  2. A PreToolUse hook checks today's measured spend before the skill runs.
  3. Over the cap: exit 2, the skill is blocked — not a suggestion, an enforced gate.

Playbook

Three plays

  1. Tier the models. Match model and effort to the job — not the most capable setting by default.
  2. Pool the spend. One visible number your team can see, not N invisible personal ones.
  3. Codify the top loops. Demo 1 → Demo 2 — pinned, versioned, gated.

Exercise

Your turn — 10 minutes, pairs

  1. Pick one task your team repeats weekly.
  2. What does "explore" look like for it, and what would "codify" fix in place?
  3. Which model/effort does each half actually need?
